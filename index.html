Overview

行程總覽

The arc行程脈絡

Four cities, coast to coast. Architecture and a ballgame in Chicago, museums and theatre in New York, the coast and country music in San Diego, then a Pacific finish over Los Angeles and Santa Monica before the late flight home. 橫跨全國的四座城市。芝加哥的建築與棒球、紐約的美術館與劇場、聖地牙哥的海岸與鄉村音樂,最後在洛杉磯與聖塔莫尼卡看著太平洋收尾,再搭深夜班機返家。

The thread貫穿主軸

The James Beard Media Awards, jazz at the Green Mill, the Met with a guide, MJ the Musical, Pacific Surfliner up the coast, EVA Royal Laurel home. Culture and craft, not a checklist. James Beard 媒體獎、Green Mill 的爵士樂、由導覽帶看的大都會博物館、《MJ 音樂劇》、太平洋衝浪者號沿岸北上、長榮皇璽桂冠艙返家。文化與工藝,而非清單打卡。

The finish收尾

Up the coast by rail to Los Angeles — Reggae Night at the Hollywood Bowl with Griffith city lights after, the Getty Villa and Santa Monica bluffs, K-town dinner at Park's BBQ, then a direct late drive to LAX for the 00:15 EVA flight. 搭火車沿岸北上洛杉磯 — 好萊塢露天劇場的雷鬼之夜、隨後格里斐斯的城市夜景、蓋蒂別墅與聖塔莫尼卡崖邊、韓國城 Park's BBQ 晚餐,再直接深夜驅車前往 LAX 搭 00:15 長榮班機。
